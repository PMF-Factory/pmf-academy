FAQ

Questions, answered

Who are the organizers?

The Growth Academy is organized by PMF Factory and NZZ Connect.

How do I join?

Request information through the form. Shortlisted companies are invited to a virtual selection interview.

Where does it take place?

Modules alternate between in-person sessions in Zürich and online workshops, on Friday afternoons from 13:30 to 17:30.

What are the selection criteria?

At least CHF 100,000 in annual revenue, the ambition to grow and learn, and two team members who take part actively. Only 10 startups are accepted per academy.

Excited to join?

Book an interview and find out if it’s a fit.

Only 10 startups are selected. Other questions? Write to academy@pmf-factory.com
