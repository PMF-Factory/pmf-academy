FAQ

Questions, answered

Who runs Swiss PMF Academy?

The academy is powered by SICTIC, the Swiss ICT Investor Club, and PMF Factory.

How is the program structured?

Six months: two days of intensive, hands-on workshops, seven guided sprints with expert input and actionable feedback, a founders dinner and an Investor Demo Day.

Who should take part from my company?

Two key people from your leadership team, both committed to taking part throughout the program.

What does it cost?

CHF 4,250 per participant, so CHF 8,500 per company. A partner discount and an early-bird discount (until January 15) of CHF 1,000 each bring the total to CHF 6,500.

Is my startup a fit?

Typically B2B, 2–7 years on the market, CHF 200k–2.1M in revenue (at least CHF 50k), first customers and real usage data, and a growth challenge or plateau.

How do I apply?

Book a call. We look at where you stand and whether the academy is the right next step for you.
