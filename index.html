Product-Market Fit is what makes a company fast and efficient

Startups with PMF win customers more easily, grow faster and attract more investors. They have the momentum on their side to grow fast.

Signals of Product-Market Fit

Sales become predictable

Customer acquisition costs are dropping

What Product-Market Fit is NOT

  • Prospects keep delaying their buying decisions.
  • Sales just feels hard.
  • Every customer project is different, with multiple use cases.
  • Existing customers don’t ask for more.

Testimonials

What founders say

Joel Singh · CEO, Crossiety

Side note: Crossiety is growing again and is profitable.

Interview

The program was an absolute game changer for us

What impact did the systematic work on Product-Market Fit have?
Joel: It fundamentally changed how we set our priorities. Today we make decisions based on what really matters to our users – not simply on what feels most urgent at the moment.
What changed in sales?
Joel: We now have a clearly defined Ideal Customer Profile. That means we talk to the right customers in a targeted way instead of approaching as many different prospects as possible. It makes our sales more focused and more efficient.
What impact did this have on your product development?
Joel: We used to get a lot of technical feedback, but often it was individual opinions or very specific requests. Today we understand much better what the majority of our users really need. That helps us prioritize our backlog and align our sprints consistently with customer value.
Has collaboration within the team changed as well?
Joel: Yes, significantly. The whole team has much more clarity today. Everyone understands better what matters to our users, so we work in a more focused way and pursue the same priorities. Our meetings have a different quality, too: we no longer just discuss ideas and opinions, we show data and customer signals. That builds trust and leads to better decisions.
What did the process do for you personally as a founder?
Joel: It gave me much more confidence. I feel I finally have a compass. I know better where we are going – and above all, why we are taking this path.

The program runs from March to August. In-person days take place in Zurich; the other sprints are online. There are about three weeks between sprints.

How is the program structured?

Two kick-off days give you the essential tools, methods and frameworks. In seven growth sprints, you experiment, acquire customers and learn from experts and peers. At the Demo Day, you showcase your progress and pitch to investors.

Who are the guest speakers?

Experienced founders and investors such as Thomas Dübendorfer (SICTIC), Tobias Rein (GetYourGuide), Raphael Schaad (Cron, YC), Thomas Gabathuler (Get More Brain) and Stefan Zanetti (Allthings, Pivotal) share their insights on Product-Market Fit.

Who runs Swiss PMF Academy?

The academy is powered by SICTIC, the Swiss ICT Investor Club, and PMF Factory, and supported by Innovation Zurich.

Is my startup a fit?

Typical participants are B2B (occasionally B2C), 2–7 years on the market, with CHF 200k–2.1M in revenue. You should not apply if your revenue is below CHF 50,000, you have less than six months of runway, or you are a one-(wo)man show.

How many startups are selected?

Only 10 carefully selected startups take part, so every team gets real attention from experts and peers.

Who should take part from my company?

Two key people from your leadership team, both committed to taking part throughout the six months.

What does it cost?

CHF 4,250 per participant, so CHF 8,500 per company. A partner discount (SICTIC, Standortförderung Kanton Zürich, PMF Factory) and an early-bird discount until January 15 of CHF 1,000 each bring the total to CHF 6,500.
